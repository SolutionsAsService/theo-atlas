ⓘ How to read this dashboard+

A reference price, not a consolidated execution price. The headline uses the most liquid live, verified tracked DEX pool with a USD price. MEXC pricing and volume remain in USDT; no USD conversion or stablecoin parity is assumed.

Pool liquidity and order books are different. DEX liquidity is the sum of live, uniquely identified verified pools. MEXC depth is the value of resting bids and asks within ±2% of its mid-price, denominated in USDT. It is not added to DEX liquidity.

Coverage is bounded by the sources. This is a view of discovered and configured markets, not a guarantee of every listing. Discovered, unverified pools are visibly flagged and excluded from headline totals. Robinhood refers to the network label returned for a pool, not an assertion that THEO trades on Robinhood’s brokerage. Stale and unavailable markets are excluded from headline totals. Source-provided 24-hour volumes are not a measure of unique traders.

Freshness and listing age. Fetched timestamps show when the server retrieved a source, not the time of the last trade or an on-chain reserve update. Observations more than two minutes old are marked stale. MEXC’s change is measured from its reported opening price; a new listing can have less than 24 hours of history.

No invented history. The chart uses returned MEXC history or real observations if the source provides them. Missing values stay empty. Refresh checks the server cache; upstream sources may update on their own schedule. This dashboard is read-only and does not connect wallets or place trades.
